What is Gift Nifty?

Gift Nifty is a futures contract based on India's benchmark index â€” the NIFTY 50. It is traded on the NSE International Exchange (NSE IFSC) located in GIFT City (Gujarat International Finance Tec-City).

History of Gift Nifty

Before 2023, international investors who wanted exposure to Indian markets commonly traded the SGX Nifty in Singapore. SGX Nifty became popular because it allowed global participation, provided longer trading hours, and reflected global market sentiment.

To increase domestic control and deepen liquidity inside India, the NSE shifted the contract to NSE IFSC in GIFT City. From July 3, 2023, SGX Nifty officially became Gift Nifty â€” a notable milestone in India's financial development strategy.

Why Gift Nifty is Important for Indian Traders

Gift Nifty trades for extended hours (roughly 21 hours a day across global sessions). Because of these extended hours, it often reflects:

Example: If Nifty closed at 22,000 and Gift Nifty is trading at 22,150 before Indian markets open, that usually signals a gap-up opening of roughly 150 points. Traders use this to plan Nifty futures or options positions before 9:15 AM.
