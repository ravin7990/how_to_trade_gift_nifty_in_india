Gift Nifty

Gift Nifty Trading Hours in India

A compact session guide for traders who want to know when Gift Nifty moves and how that relates to the Indian market open.

Why the Session Matters

Gift Nifty is watched closely because it can give traders a hint about the opening tone for the Indian market. The morning and overnight windows are especially useful for reading sentiment.

How Traders Use It

  • Compare the current level with the previous Nifty close.
  • Watch the morning move before 9:15 AM IST.
  • Use it to plan the day rather than chase the open blindly.

Gift Nifty Hours FAQ

When does Gift Nifty matter most?

It matters most during the pre-open period and when global markets are moving overnight.

Why do Indian traders watch the morning move?

Because it can help set expectations for the opening gap and the first part of the trading session.
