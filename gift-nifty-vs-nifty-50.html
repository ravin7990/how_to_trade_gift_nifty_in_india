Gift Nifty

Gift Nifty vs Nifty 50

A simple comparison for Indian traders who want to understand what each instrument means and when to use it.

Quick Difference

Nifty 50 is the benchmark equity index for India. Gift Nifty is a futures contract tied to the Nifty 50 that trades on NSE IFSC in GIFT City. Traders often watch Gift Nifty to read early market sentiment before the Indian open.

Why It Matters

  • Nifty 50 reflects the Indian stock market.
  • Gift Nifty reflects extended-hour sentiment around Nifty.
  • Gift Nifty can help traders plan opening moves and gap expectations.

Gift Nifty vs Nifty 50 FAQ

Are Gift Nifty and Nifty 50 the same?

No. Nifty 50 is the Indian equity index, while Gift Nifty is a futures contract linked to that index.

Why do traders watch Gift Nifty before the open?

It often reflects overnight global sentiment and can help traders estimate whether the Indian market may open with a gap.
