Best Time to Trade US Futures From India

A session-based guide for Indian traders who want to know when Dow, S&P 500 and other US futures are most active.

Best Window

The most active window is usually the US evening session in India, when US markets are open and liquidity is strongest.

What to Watch

  • US economic releases
  • Opening volatility in New York
  • Major news headlines and Fed events

US Futures FAQ

What is the best time to trade US futures from India?

The evening and late-night hours in IST are usually the most active because they overlap with the US regular session.

Which markets does this apply to?

It applies to major US futures such as Dow Futures and the S&P 500, plus other globally traded US contracts.
