Gift Nifty Margin Guide

A beginner-friendly overview of margin, leverage and risk when trading Gift Nifty.

What Margin Means

Margin is the capital a broker requires you to keep in the account before you can open a futures position. Because futures are leveraged, the margin is smaller than the full contract value, but risk is still significant.

Risk Rules to Remember

  • Use a stop-loss on every trade.
  • Do not use the full account balance on one position.
  • Keep position size small while learning.

Gift Nifty Margin FAQ

Why does futures trading need margin?

Because you are trading a leveraged contract, not the full cash value upfront.

Is lower margin always better?

No. Lower margin can mean higher leverage, which also increases risk.
